# Hungry Hole

Family-only, not for sale. A LANDSCAPE browser game: drag a hole around huge places, eat things smaller than you, grow, climb each world's objective ladder, finish Tiny Quests, earn coins, buy per-world upgrades.

- **One public repo** — build and play here. Live: https://zerononeex.github.io/hungry-hole/ (`index.html` + the vendored `three.min.js` at the repo root; merging to `main` publishes).
- **The worlds (v5):** five — The Home · Stanley Park · Metro Vancouver · The Globe · Space (v5.2 order) — from one Lego brick to swallowing Everest. Four modes on every world: STORY, I SPY, EDUCATIONAL, EXPLORATION.
- **Spec:** `docs/SPEC.md` (v2, owner restructure applied). Builders: branch, build, Kakashi verifies BEFORE merge.
- **v3 (3D):** Three.js r147 vendored as `three.min.js` (no CDN, works offline). Builder decisions in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Tests: `python3 -m http.server 8765` at the root, then `node tests/{data,behaviour,audio,reachability,ui-flow,balance-bot,screens,perf}.js`.
- **v4 (owner feedback batch):** true-scale Ch1–4 (metre table), Ch3 baked from OpenStreetMap data (`tools/bake-ch3.py` → `data/ch3-osm.js`, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), STORY / I SPY / EDUCATIONAL modes, audio slots in `audio/` (prompts in `docs/AUDIO_PROMPTS.md`). Decisions: `docs/DECISIONS.md` (v4 section).
- **v4.1:** music plays through WebAudio (fixes silent music on iPhone; `HH.debugAudio()` reports what is playing), text-to-speech removed game-wide, and an orange UPGRADES button in the HUD. Notes: `docs/DECISIONS.md` (v4.1 section).
- **v5 (the worlds redo):** five worlds (Wilderness and Chaos retired; old saves keep their data aside in `S.retired`), objective ladder with a growth cap in every mode, zone rings, minimap + big map, Exploration, a living Vancouver title. The Home from the owner's floorplan (layout only), Stanley Park from OpenStreetMap inside the owner's boundary (`tools/bake-stanley.py` → `data/stanley.js`), and the Globe on true-scale Natural Earth data (public domain; `tools/bake-globe.py` → `data/globe.js`, landmarks in `data/globe-landmarks.js`). Decisions, per-world density, pace table and the co-op status: `docs/DECISIONS.md` (v5 section). Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/`.
- **v5.2 (the Globe becomes a school, plus the fix batch):** world order Home · Stanley Park · Metro Vancouver · Globe · Space (one-time save move, each world keeps its song). The Globe gets borders, capitals and names by size tier, the country readout, every country's animals from the owner's `world_animals.xlsx` (`tools/bake-animals.py` + `tools/animal_families.py` → `data/globe-animals.js`) and his landmark roster from `world_195_countries_famous_landmarks.xlsx` (`tools/bake-landmarks.py` + `tools/landmarks/geo-*.txt` → `data/globe-landmarks52.js`). Real ground height for Stanley Park and Metro Vancouver from the public Terrain Tiles (`tools/bake-elevation.py` → `data/elevation.js`) with eatable hills, and real stairs in the Home. New suite: `node tests/globe-school.js`. Decisions and the test table: `docs/DECISIONS.md` (v5.2 section).
- **v5.3 (multiplayer grows up):** up to four players on one room code (a star through the host; each friend in their own colour, by name, arrow and minimap dot; friends can join a game already going), the I Spy win counted on every player's save, Upgrades that pause nobody in multiplayer, no zone rings in multiplayer, one shared team size in multiplayer Story, I Spy hands of one size played at that size, Educational sets at their biggest target's size plus one step with a speed dial, every upgrade unlocked in both (except the coin upgrades), and animal hunts on the Globe. Decisions, the no-kid-falls-behind audit and the test table: `docs/DECISIONS.md` (v5.3 section). Fix round 1: no I Spy hand is ever a single target, the mini globe no longer glows gold where you have been, and a slow iPad loading the world is no longer dropped as "lost".
