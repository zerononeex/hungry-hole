# Hungry Hole

Family-only, not for sale. A portrait browser game for the kids: drag a hole around, eat things smaller than you, grow, complete each level's main goal, and tick off its two side quests for stars.

- **Worlds:** Toy Cleanup, Space Hole, Treasure Hole, City — 5 levels each, 20 levels, 60 stars.
- **Spec:** see `docs/SPEC.md` (locked v1 design, level table, quest system).
- **Build:** builders work here on a branch, ship a finished `site/`, and end with `SITE READY TO MIRROR`. Kakashi verifies, merges, and mirrors to the play repo.
- **Play mirror (never build here):** `zerononeex/hungry-hole-play`
